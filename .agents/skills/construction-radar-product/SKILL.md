# Construction Radar Product

Protect the product promise: fewer signals, clearer business relevance, concrete actions, and verifiable sources.

## Start here

1. Read [references/product-contract.md](references/product-contract.md).
2. Read `../../../docs/PROJECT_CONTEXT.md` for current state and resolved ambiguities.
3. Read `../../../docs/product/PRODUCT_BRIEF.md` for scope and success metrics.
4. For scheduling or milestone decisions, also read `../../../ROADMAP.md` and `../../../docs/quality/QUALITY_GATES.md`.

## Workflow

1. Name the user decision or evidence the requested work should improve.
2. Classify the proposal as current MVP, post-G4, post-G5, or post-pilot.
3. Check source rights, two-person capacity, and whether the idea strengthens Construction/HoReCa before expanding scope.
4. Define an observable outcome, owner, metric, and applicable gate.
5. Prefer the smallest experiment that tests the assumption with real users.
6. Update the roadmap or product contract only when the decision changes approved scope, ownership, KPI, or sequencing.

Do not treat audience size, source count, model size, or content volume as success by themselves. Favor evidence that a user trusted a card and took a useful action.
