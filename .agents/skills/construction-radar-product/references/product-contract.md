# Product contract quick reference

## Promise

Turn permitted external business signals into a personalized explanation of what happened, why it matters, what to do, and where the facts came from.

## First market

- Verticals: Construction and HoReCa only through Gate G4.
- Interface: Telegram bot.
- Closed MVP: 20-50 target users in two cohorts.
- Baseline sources: 20-40 stable, permitted sources; 40-60 is a stretch target. Do not scale to 100+ before quality evidence.

## Owners

- Артём: product and engineering critical path.
- Денис: sources/permissions, market feedback, content, users, and physical computer availability.

## Hard boundaries

- No mass unauthorized Telegram scraping.
- No AI processing without explicit permission status.
- No CRM/ERP, billing, mobile app, broad portal, or new vertical before the current MVP proves useful.
- No fine-tuning without a stable, measured gap after prompt/rules/evals.
- No product claim that exceeds working behavior.

## Outcome hierarchy

1. User takes a valuable action after a card.
2. Card is judged useful and trustworthy.
3. The right signals arrive reliably and on time.
4. Audience and source count support distribution but do not replace product evidence.

## Gate thresholds

- Pipeline: uptime >95%, JSON success >98%, obvious duplicates <5%.
- Product: high-score precision >70%, positive feedback >60%, source links 100%, feedback coverage >20%, unsupported factual claims toward zero.
- Commercial: self-service onboarding/digest/source/feedback, 20-50 closed-MVP users, outcome stories, and explicit willingness-to-pay evidence.
